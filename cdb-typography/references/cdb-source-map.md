## Distilled Rules

- Typography is shape design plus reading design.
- Text roles must be clear before choosing decorative font style.
- Readability depends on font, size, weight, line height, spacing, alignment, and output medium.
- Slide text should avoid large paragraphs unless the slide is intentionally for reading.
- Mixed Chinese/English layouts require alignment and visual-weight checks.
