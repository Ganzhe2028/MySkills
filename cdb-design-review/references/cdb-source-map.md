## Distilled Rules

- A design review starts from task, audience, message, and output context.
- Functionality fails when the design does not help the intended audience understand or act.
- Efficiency fails when the viewer must spend unnecessary effort to find hierarchy, relation, or sequence.
- Emotion fails when mood, style, or decoration competes with communication.
- Visual consistency matters most when multiple elements form a system, such as icon sets or repeated slides.
