# CDB Image Shape

Use this skill when a design depends on images, shapes, icons, raster files, vector files, or print/screen asset quality.

## Core Position

Images and shapes are not filler. They must explain, identify, classify, prove, or evoke something useful for the audience.

## Workflow

1. Identify the asset job:
   - explain
   - identify
   - categorize
   - prove
   - evoke emotion
   - decorate
2. Choose the asset type:
   - bitmap/raster for photographic or pixel-based images
   - vector/shape for scalable icons, symbols, diagrams, and simple forms
   - hybrid when photo plus vector annotation is needed
3. Check output context: screen, print, slide, poster, icon, or UI.
4. For bitmap, check crop, subject, resolution, unwanted elements, and color fit.
5. For vector or icon systems, check style, stroke, perspective, semantic, detail, and grid consistency.
6. Give concrete revision steps.

## Checks

- Do not recommend a bitmap for a scalable icon unless there is a clear reason.
- Do not approve low-resolution images for print.
- Do not judge icons only by beauty; judge quick recognition and system consistency.
- Do not let background detail compete with the subject.
