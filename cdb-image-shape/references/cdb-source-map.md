## Distilled Rules

- Bitmap is appropriate for photographic or pixel-based imagery; vector is appropriate for scalable symbols, icons, and shape systems.
- Print work requires resolution checks before approval.
- Image crop and scale should strengthen the subject and remove noise.
- Icon systems must maintain style, stroke, perspective, semantic, detail, and grid consistency.
- Shapes should communicate function quickly at target size.
